What is your role as an employee?

Although technology plays a significant role you however are ultimately responsible for the ERP implementations success. What will turn this implementation from just installing new software into a genuine advantage for ABC Holdings is your willingness to participate and learn. Here is how you can contribute significantly:


1. Attend and Participate in Training

We understand the transition from paper-based system to a new ERP one will be difficult. To help with this there will be role-specific training provided that are designed for you and your job specifically, this is because the training includes role-specific training that covers only the tasks and screens you would encounter on a daily basis at work. Your active participation is required so whenever necessary interact with trainers, ask questions and attend training sessions. To know when you are scheduled to be in training please refer to the resource page to find out.


2. Be Open Minded and have Patience

Change can be scary and difficult, so have patience and an open mind when approaching it. Be patient with yourself and your colleagues as we all learn together. Everyone is different and not everybody is able to grasp new concepts at the same pace, so be understanding and patient with one another. Open your mind and approach this new ERP system with a positive mindset and half of the work will already be done.


3. Use the Support Systems

If you encounter a problem, please let us know! Contact the ERP Helpdesk. Your questions help us to identify areas where we need to provide more guidance or improve the system. There is no such thing as a "stupid" question. Asking questions helps you understand and learn. The ERP Helpdesk can be reached over phone or email and their contact information as well as operating hours can be found on the Resource page or by clicking on the button below.


4. Provide Honest Feedback

We would love to hear from you! Providing feedback on your progress and concerns will help us. This feedback could be used to improve systems and make the process smoother for other employees. There will be suggestion boxes and surveys available for you to leave your feedback to us.


5. Be a Champion

If you find the system helpful, share your tips and successes with your team. Helping your fellow colleagues builds a supportive community and speeds up the adoption process of the system for everyone.


By following these simple guidelines, together we are able to transform these initial challenges into long-term success for both you and ABC Holdings.
