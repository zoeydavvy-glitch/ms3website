Fequently Asked Questions


Will the ERP implementation lead to job losses?

No, the purpose of the ERP is to eliminate routine tasks, not to take the position of our dedicated employees. The goal is to free up your time so you are able to concentrate on more crucial duties. It is a tool to help you, not to replace you

Why are we implementing a new system?

The new ERP system is a strategic investment in our future, even if our current procedures have worked well for us. By bringing different departments together on a single platform, it will increase our efficiency and reduce errors which will make for better decision making.

Will the new system be hard to learn?

We acknowledge that learning a new system can be daunting. For this reason, we have created role-specific training that covers only the tasks and screens you would encounter on a daily basis at work. Additionally, the training will include hands on practice and simulations to build up your confidence. Remember support will be available every step of the way and its okay to learn at your own pace.

What will happen to all our existing data? Will it be transferred safely?

Yes, safeguarding our data is a top priority. We have detailed a plan for data cleansing and validation before our data is moved. To guarantee a safe and secure transfer, the migration will be carried out meticulously during the staggered deployment, it will begin with parts of the system that are not critical to daily operations, then pilot test each phase with a small group before full deployment.

What if i struggle with the system after initial training?

You will not be left alone! After the system is online, we will to set up a dedicated ERP Helpdesk to offer prompt support. There will also be feedback channels to address ongoing concerns. Please do not hesitate to ask for help as it is the best way for you to learn.

What if I find a problem or have a suggestion for improving the new system?

We want to hear from you! Your feedback is essential. We will set up feedback channels such as surveys and suggestion boxes to capture your concerns and ideas. This will help us improve the system for everyone

Will this new system create more work for me, especially during the transition?

Our goal is the opposite. We're introducing the ERP system to simplify your work in the long run. However, we anticipate a short stabilization period immediately after launch where some tasks might take a little longer as we all get used to the new workflows. We have planned for this, and performance expectations will be adjusted during this time. The short-term effort will lead to long-term ease.
